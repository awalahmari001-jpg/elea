Authentic IPA Phoneme Audio Samples — Attribution
==================================================

The 32 MP3 phoneme reference clips in this directory are loudness-normalized,
trimmed, MP3-encoded derivatives of the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)
audio chart recordings hosted on Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0 and
Public Domain licenses:

- Vowels (/æ/, /ɛ/, /ɪ/, /ɒ/, /ʌ/, /iː/, /uː/):
  Wikimedia Commons IPA Vowel Chart recordings (User:Denelson83 & contributors,
  CC BY-SA 3.0).
- Consonants & Digraphs (/b/, /k/, /d/, /f/, /ɡ/, /h/, /dʒ/, /l/, /m/, /n/,
  /p/, /r/, /s/, /t/, /v/, /w/, /j/, /z/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, /θ/, /ð/, /ŋ/, and
  clusters /kw/, /ks/):
  Wikimedia Commons IPA Pulmonic Consonant & Affricate Chart recordings
  (User:Peter Isotalo & contributors, CC BY-SA 3.0 / Public Domain).
